# Legacy capabilities retained selectively

The older archives used an obligatory seven-stage expert debate, a keyword-to-persona generator and universal text/PDF packages. The current router and modular references preserve the useful *behaviors* without mandating costly ceremony. Load this file only for a DEEP or legacy-compatible analysis, audience adaptation or research-grade report. It does not claim that simulated roles become independent people or that legacy benchmark percentages apply to this skill.

## Optional deep deliberation (adapted from v8–v24)

1. Capture separate first-pass cards before critique; keep evidence and doubts visible. For a single-model web chat, describe them as sequential perspectives.
2. List points of overlap only where the claims themselves match; do not convert agreement into proof. Record missing viewpoints.
3. Classify each material dispute: factual (verify externally), priority/value (surface the user's criterion), or approach/implementation (compare alternatives and run a reversible experiment if authorized). Include dissent when the evidence remains mixed.
4. Revise a claim only when evidence warrants it; keep the initial card and state what changed. Compare the final synthesis against individual cards to recover a decision-changing minority risk that got diluted.
5. For consequential work, run a concrete pre-mortem: assume the selected plan fails, identify one plausible causal chain, observable early warning and mitigation. Do not label every imagined failure critical. Stop when another pass brings no new decision-relevant evidence.

If an extensive record is explicitly requested, use `assets/deep_review_template.md` for role cards, the dispute log, evidence ledger and an action plan. Otherwise use the compact report.

## Audience adaptation

Honor explicit `TLDR`, `ELI5`, `Feynman`, `Jargonize`, `Humanize`, or `Sources` requests without compromising safety or truth. TLDR keeps recommendation and its most material qualification; ELI5 defines terms and uses accurate simple analogies; Feynman first explains simply, then shows a concrete example and unresolved gap; technical style uses precise terms; Sources includes only sources actually consulted with URLs or artifact locations. Do not fabricate a fixed number of citations or present a metaphor as fact. A novice explanation can include an observable first step rather than simply shortening text.

## Research and findings

For a DEEP research task, keep hypotheses, disconfirmation criteria, source dates and traceable claim IDs. Track a hypothesis as tested, unresolved or refuted; unsupported agreement stays tentative. Provide reproducible checks when feasible; a promised or proposed search is NOT_RUN until actually done. Preserve the older finding registry's useful distinction between severity, evidence, owner and timed action without inventing a deadline or owner. The current `references/evidence_and_research.md` remains the source of confidence definitions.

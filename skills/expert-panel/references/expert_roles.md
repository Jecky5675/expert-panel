# Adaptive Expert Roles

Use roles as analytical functions, not fictional authorities. Build the smallest panel that covers distinct failure models and cognitive operations.

## Mandatory functions

| Function | Purpose | Must not do |
|---|---|---|
| Orchestrator | Scope, route, enforce independence, stop loops | Decide based on prestige or verbosity |
| Domain Expert(s) | Apply relevant technical/professional knowledge | Invent current facts or cite the persona as evidence |
| Evidence Auditor | Check source quality, dates, independence, contradictions | Vote based on eloquence |
| Adversary | Attack assumptions, incentives, misuse, and failure paths | Manufacture objections without decision relevance |
| Synthesizer | Integrate evidence and trade-offs | Erase dissent or average incompatible claims |

## Optional functions

- **User Advocate:** usability, accessibility, adoption, burden, and affected people.
- **Implementation Lead:** dependencies, sequencing, operational feasibility, rollback.
- **Statistician / Methodologist:** measurement, sampling, base rates, uncertainty, causal claims.
- **Security / Privacy:** threat model, attack surface, least privilege, data handling.
- **Legal / Compliance:** jurisdiction, obligations, enforceability, evidence of current law.
- **Cost / Operations:** total cost, staffing, maintenance, capacity, failure recovery.
- **Scenario Planner:** drivers, branches, leading indicators, trigger points.
- **Prompt / Harness Engineer:** context, tool permissions, memory, evals, failure containment.
- **Mediator:** interests, acceptable trade zones, commitments, escalation paths.
- **README Information Architect:** audience, reading order, proof placement, first successful action, content compression.
- **Maintainer / Developer Advocate:** technical accuracy, installation path, examples, maintenance burden, contributor needs.
- **Visual Systems Designer:** project-native art direction, typography, composition, motif, scalable asset system.
- **Accessibility / SVG Reviewer:** alt text, contrast, readable sizes, sanitizer compatibility, motion restraint, reduced-motion fallback.

## Dynamic role card

```text
ROLE:
DOMAIN:
REASONING OPERATION:
UNIQUE LENS:
KEY QUESTION:
REQUIRED EVIDENCE:
LIKELY BIAS/BLIND SPOT:
DEPENDENCIES WITH OTHER ROLES:
STOP CONDITION:
```

## Diversity test

Reject or merge a role when it cannot answer all three:

1. What distinct cognitive operation does this role perform?
2. What unique failure could this role detect?
3. What evidence would only or especially matter to this role?
4. What recommendation might this role make that another role would reasonably oppose?

## Panel sizing

- QUICK: Orchestrator, 1–2 domain lenses, Adversary/Synthesizer combined only for low-risk work.
- STANDARD: 3–4 domain lenses plus separate Evidence Auditor, Adversary, Synthesizer.
- DEEP: 4–7 domain lenses plus the three control functions; split into workstreams rather than exceeding ten active voices.

## Bias controls

- Blind first pass before peer exposure.
- Remove role/model identity from peer cards and randomize review order when possible.
- Do not show confidence scores until after initial claims.
- Evidence Auditor evaluates claim-source links, not authors.
- A high-status or rhetorically strong role gets no extra weight.
- Record whether a role changed its view and what evidence caused the change.
- Ask every review round what all cards missed and flag high-overlap agreement as possible theatrical consensus.

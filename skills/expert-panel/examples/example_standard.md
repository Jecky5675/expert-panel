# Example: Standard Architecture Decision

**Request:** “Run the expert panel on whether our team should split a monolith into microservices this year.”

Expected routing:

- STANDARD + PLAN + VERIFY
- Architecture, operations/SRE, security, product/delivery, cost, Evidence Auditor, Adversary, Synthesizer
- Blind claim cards, then sparse critiques on reliability, team capacity, and migration risk
- Decision matrix with hard constraints, incremental alternative, pre-mortem, and measurable next experiment

The answer should not assume microservices are inherently modern or superior. It should identify the conditions under which decomposition is justified and recommend a reversible first step.
